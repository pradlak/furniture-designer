Online wardrobe and built-in furniture designer — free, in 3D

Meble Samemu is a free furniture design program that runs in your browser. Use it to design a built-in wardrobe for an alcove, attic storage under a sloped ceiling, kitchen cabinets with a worktop, a bookcase or a chest of drawers — then download a cut list and order the boards cut to size. No installation and no sign-up.

What you can design

Made-to-measure wardrobe

Enter the alcove size and add cabinets, shelves, doors and drawers. The program divides the alcove into cabinets, places the hinges and keeps a clearance under the ceiling, while fillers cover gaps at uneven walls.

Attic storage under a sloped ceiling

Enter the knee wall and the slope — cabinets under the roof get a sloped top and sides, and the cut list includes the angled cuts with dimensions.

Kitchen cabinets

Base and wall cabinets, drawers, a worktop with cut-outs for the sink and an induction or gas hob, a plinth, a cooker hood and tiles above the worktop — all in 3D.

Cut list for panel cutting

Cutting dimensions allowing for edge banding, quantities, machining and accessories (hinges, runners, legs). Excel, CSV and a ready-made file for the meble.pl cutting service, plus drawings of fronts and shelves with holes.

How it works

  1. The space — a rectangular alcove, an attic with a sloped ceiling or any wall shape; obstacles such as a cooker hood, a pipe or a fireplace.
  2. Furniture in 3D — cabinets, shelves, doors, drawers, fillers and worktops; change the dimensions with the mouse or by typing numbers.
  3. Cut list and order — download the list of panels to cut and order the cutting from a board supplier or at meble.pl.

Frequently asked questions

Is the furniture designer free?

Yes. The program runs in your browser, is completely free and needs no installation.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Your project is saved in your browser and you can export it to a file. Signing in with Google is optional — it is handy when you want your projects on several computers.

How do I order panel cutting?

Download the cut list in Excel or CSV and take it to a board supplier with a cutting service, or load the ready-made file into the cutting configurator at meble.pl (a Polish online cutting service). The list contains cutting dimensions, quantities and edge banding.

What board thickness should I use?

For cabinets and shelves most often 18 mm melamine-faced chipboard, 3 mm HDF for the backs and 28–38 mm for worktops. You choose the material and thickness in the program and the cut list updates automatically.

Does the program work on a phone?

It works in any modern browser, but a computer with a mouse is the most comfortable for designing.
