Free online furniture designer

Design an alcove wardrobe, attic furniture or kitchen cabinets in 3D, then download a cut list to order panel cutting. No sign-up, no installation — in your browser.

Who is this program for?

For people who want to make furniture themselves: order cut boards from a board supplier or a shop with a cutting service and assemble a wardrobe or built-in unit at home. The program keeps track of the dimensions for you — it calculates the side panels, top and bottom panels, shelves, backs, fronts and fillers, and finally gives you a ready list of boards to cut.

How it works — from the alcove to the cut list

  1. Space. Enter the dimensions of the alcove or room, mark the walls, add slopes (attic, knee wall) and obstacles such as pipes or skirting boards.
  2. Cabinets. Add cabinets — the program splits the alcove into equal parts. Set the height, depth, back (HDF or board), legs, rails and shelves.
  3. Fillers and worktop. Side fillers at the walls, a wall panel, a bottom filler (the plinth hiding the legs), a top filler up to the ceiling and a worktop.
  4. Fronts. Single or double doors or drawers, with an adjustable gap, a handle groove or decorative routing, and handles.
  5. Cut list. Download the list of boards to cut: Excel, CSV or a file to load into the cutting service on meble.pl (a Polish panel-cutting service).

What the designer can do

  • 3D view — rotate, zoom, move cabinets with the mouse, open doors and drawers.
  • Dimensions in 3D — select a cabinet, filler or legs to see their dimensions; select several items to see the overall dimensions.
  • Attic with a slope — cabinets with a sloped top cut to the angle of the slope, fillers up to the ceiling and to the slope.
  • Links — change the width, height, depth or legs of several cabinets at once.
  • Materials and edge banding — melamine-faced chipboard, lacquered MDF, HDF, worktop; thicknesses, colours and edge banding on selected edges.
  • Calculations in fields — type e.g. 1100-70 and the program works out the result.

Cut list for panel cutting

A cut piece is a rectangular piece of board that you order from a cutting service. The program collects all the boards of the project into one list: material, thickness, cutting dimension (reduced by the edge banding), quantity, which edges to band, as well as angled cuts, routing and handle holes. Identical pieces are merged into one line. You also get a separate list of accessories: legs, hinges, drawer runners and handles.

Frequently asked questions

Is the furniture designer free?

Yes. The program runs in your browser, is free of charge and needs no sign-up or installation.

Where is my project saved?

The project is saved automatically in your browser. To keep it longer or move it to another computer, use the Export button (a .json file) and then Import.

Can I order panel cutting based on the design?

Yes. The Cut list button saves the list of boards to cut in Excel or CSV — with cutting dimensions, edge banding and quantities — and a file ready to load into the cutting service on meble.pl (a Polish panel-cutting service).

Does the program support attics with a sloped ceiling?

Yes. You can add slopes, a knee wall and obstacles, and cabinets under the slope get a sloped top cut to the angle of the slope. See the guide to attic furniture.
