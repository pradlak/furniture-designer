Cut list for panel cutting — generate it from a 3D design

Updated: 2026-11-27

Working out the cut list by hand is the most common source of mistakes in DIY furniture: a forgotten board thickness, wrong edge banding, a missing top panel. In Meble Samemu the cut list is created automatically from the 3D design — change the size of the wardrobe and all boards are recalculated at once.

What the cut list contains

  • Material and thickness of every board (melamine-faced chipboard, MDF for fronts, HDF for backs, worktops).
  • Cutting dimensions — taking the edge banding thickness into account, with identical pieces merged into one line.
  • Edge banding — which edges to band (along the length and the width).
  • Machining — angled cuts for slopes, routing, holes in the worktop, hinge holes in doors, shelf supports.
  • Accessories — hinges, drawer runners, legs, handles, shelf supports.

Download formats

  • Excel (.xlsx) — the most readable, with a separate accessories sheet and explanations.
  • CSV — for your own programs and spreadsheets.
  • File for meble.pl (a Polish panel-cutting service) — a separate file for each decor, ready to load into their cutting configurator (more in the guide cutting on meble.pl from a CSV file).
  • Drawings — fronts with hinge holes and shelves with machining dimensions (PDF / PNG).

You can download the cut list for the whole project or only for selected furniture and materials — e.g. the chipboard cabinets separately and the MDF fronts separately.

Frequently asked questions

What is a cut list?

It is a list of all the pieces to be cut from boards: material, thickness, cutting dimensions, quantity and edge banding. With such a list you order the cutting from a board supplier.

Does the program take edge banding into account in the dimensions?

Yes. The cutting dimension is reduced by the banding thickness on the banded edges, and the list shows which edges to band.

Can I order the cutting on meble.pl?

Yes. The program generates a file in the meble.pl cutting format (meble.pl is a Polish panel-cutting service) — separately for each board decor — which you load into their configurator.

Is the cut list generator free?

Yes, the whole program and downloading the cut list are free and need no sign-up.
