Attic storage with a sloped ceiling — a wardrobe under the slope

The space under a slope usually goes to waste, because off-the-shelf wardrobes don't fit it. A made-to-measure wardrobe uses all of it — you just need to measure the slope carefully and work out the angled cuts.

1. Measure the attic

  • Knee wall — the height of the vertical wall from the floor to where the slope starts.
  • Slope angle — the easiest way is to work it out from two measurements: how far the slope „comes into” the room at a given height. Typical roofs are 30–45°.
  • Width and depth of the space for the unit and the height of the room at its highest point.

2. Recreate the attic in the designer

In the designer click New… and choose Attic with sloped ceiling. Enter the dimensions, the knee wall height and the slope angle. You see the slope straight away in 3D — you can add more slopes and obstacles (e.g. a roof window, pipes) in the Space tab.

3. Cabinets with a sloped top

A cabinet placed under the slope gets a sloped top: the sides, back and fillers are cut parallel to the slope, and the top panel follows the slope. The program adjusts the cabinet height so that at the front it reaches the slope, leaving a clearance to the slope — without it the cabinet can't be put in place.

When fitting, a cabinet under a slope usually goes in from the front, tilted slightly — that's why the clearance between the cabinet and the slope matters more than under a flat ceiling. The gap is covered by a top filler cut to the angle.

4. Fillers and fronts under the slope

The side fillers and the top filler can go to the ceiling or to the slope — the program cuts them to the slope angle itself. Fronts end below the filler; in the lowest part of the unit, drawers or open shelves work better than doors.

5. A cut list with angled cuts

Boards for a slope are cut from a rectangle, and then a corner is cut off. In the designer's cut list such a part has the rectangle dimensions and a description of the cut, e.g. „corner cut A × B” — A along the length, B along the width. The cutting service will make such a cut if you describe it in the order.

Start with an example: open the ready-made example „Attic storage” — cabinets with a sloped top under a 45° slope, doors and shelves.
