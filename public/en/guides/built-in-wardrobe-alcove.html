How to design a built-in alcove wardrobe, step by step

An alcove wardrobe is one of the easiest pieces of furniture to make yourself: the walls set the dimensions, and you order the boards already cut to size. Most mistakes happen at the design stage — here is what to watch out for, one step at a time.

1. Measure the alcove (accurately, in several places)

Walls are rarely perfectly straight. Measure the width at the floor, halfway up and under the ceiling, the height at the left and right walls, and the depth at the top and bottom. Use the smallest values in your design — the wardrobe has to fit where the alcove is narrowest.

Also note everything that gets in the way: skirting boards, pipes, sockets, a light switch, a window sill. In the designer you add them as obstacles and can see straight away whether a cabinet overlaps them.

2. Recreate the alcove in the designer

In the designer click New… and choose Rectangular alcove. Enter the width, height and depth. In the Space tab, mark which walls are solid (left, right, back) and add any obstacles.

Ceiling clearance. A cabinet as tall as the alcove cannot be stood up — it will catch the ceiling as you raise it. Leave a few to a dozen or so millimetres of clearance and cover the gap with a top filler. The program has a separate „Ceiling clearance” setting and makes sure the cabinet fits.

3. Split the alcove into cabinets

In the Furniture tab add the first cabinet — it will take up the full width of the alcove. Each next one splits the alcove into equal parts. A few practical rules:

  • Cabinet width — usually 40–100 cm. Wider cabinets have sagging shelves and heavy doors.
  • Depth — for clothes on hangers about 55–60 cm (including the front); for shelves with folded clothes 40–50 cm is enough.
  • Back panel — HDF fixed on from the back is the cheapest and lightest; a board back (set in) stiffens the cabinet more.
  • Legs — on an uneven floor adjustable legs make levelling much easier. The program asks whether to keep the height of the unit (the cabinet gets shorter by the leg height) or raise the whole thing.

To change several cabinets at once, select them with Ctrl or switch on the link next to a dimension — changing the width, height or depth then applies to all linked cabinets.

4. Fillers: at the walls, at the bottom and at the top

Fillers are narrow strips of board that cover the gaps between the wardrobe and the walls. They make the wardrobe look built in, and the cabinets don't have to match crooked walls perfectly.

  • Side filler — in the plane of the fronts, at the wall. Make it oversized and scribe it on site to the shape of the wall.
  • Wall panel — a full board behind the filler, from the floor up; useful when the inside can be seen from the side.
  • Plinth (bottom filler) — hides the legs; set back from the fronts (by 2 cm by default in the program) so you don't kick it.
  • Top filler — fills the clearance under the ceiling; in the program it can reach the ceiling or a slope.

5. Shelves and fronts

Shelves are spaced evenly, and you can drag each one with the mouse in the 3D view. You choose fronts in the cabinet card: single doors, double doors or drawers. The gap between fronts matters — the program sets the side gap (5 mm by default, 2.5 mm at each side) and the vertical gap (3 mm by default) separately. The lowest front goes down to the bottom of the cabinet.

On a wardrobe with a top filler, the fronts end below the filler — the program works out their height itself.

6. Download the cut list and order panel cutting

Click 💾 Cut list… and pick a format: Excel (the easiest to read), CSV, or a file for panel cutting at meble.pl (a Polish online panel-cutting service). The list contains the cutting dimensions, quantities, edge banding on the edges and the accessories (legs, hinges, drawer runners, handles). More on how to read the list: cut lists and panel cutting.

Quickest way: open the ready-made example „Alcove wardrobe” — three floor-to-ceiling cabinets on legs, side, top and bottom fillers, doors and shelves. Change the alcove dimensions to match yours.
