Cut lists for panel cutting — how to work them out and order

Board wholesalers and shops with a panel-cutting service cut the board according to a cut list. A well-prepared list gives you furniture that goes together without any trimming. A poor one means an extra order and a few days of waiting.

What a cut piece is

A cut piece is a rectangular piece of board with specific dimensions: a wardrobe side, a top or bottom panel of a cabinet, a shelf, a rail, a front, a filler. The cut list tells the saw operator: which board, what size, how many pieces and which edges to band.

How to work out the sizes of the cut pieces

An example for a 600 × 720 × 560 mm cabinet made of 18 mm board, with a 3 mm HDF back fixed on from the back (full-height sides, top and bottom panels between the sides):

PartSize [mm]Where it comes from
Side (2 pcs)720 × 557cabinet height × depth minus the back (560 − 3)
Bottom and top panel564 × 557width minus two sides (600 − 2 × 18) × side depth
Shelf564 × 557like the top/bottom panel (it can be made shallower)
HDF back590 × 710the cabinet minus 5 mm on each side

With a dozen or so cabinets, fillers and fronts, working it out by hand is easy to get wrong. The designer works out all the cut pieces itself from the 3D model — and merges identical ones into one row with a quantity.

Edge banding: finished size and cutting size

Edge banding is a tape glued onto the edge of the board, usually 0.8–2 mm. It has its own thickness, so the cutting size is smaller than the finished size by the banding thickness on the banded edges. Example: a 564 mm shelf banded at the front with 2 mm tape is cut 562 mm deep.

The designer's list contains both values — „cutting” and „finished” — as well as the number of banded edges along the length and the width (0, 1 or 2) and their names (e.g. „front”). Usually the visible edges are banded: the fronts of the sides, top/bottom panels and shelves, and all edges of the fronts.

Ask the cutting service whether you should give the finished size (with banding) or the cutting size. Companies handle this differently — the designer's list has both sizes, so you can pick the right column.

Grain

Boards with a wood-effect decor have a grain direction — usually along the longer side of the board. If you want the grain to run vertically on the sides and fronts, say so in the order. With plain boards (e.g. white) the direction doesn't matter, and the cutting service can rotate the pieces and use less board.

Accessories

Add the hardware to your order: legs, concealed hinges (2 for low doors, 3–4 for tall ones — the designer counts them by door height), drawer runners and handles. The designer lists them separately, on the „Accessories” sheet.

How to download the cut list from the designer

  1. Design your furniture in the designer (or open an example project).
  2. Click 💾 Cut list… on the top bar.
  3. Choose a format: Excel (clear columns with units, accessories on a separate sheet), CSV (for cutting optimisation software) or meble.pl (a file to load into the panel-cutting service of meble.pl, a Polish online board shop).
  4. Tick what to export: cabinets, HDF backs, fronts, fillers, worktops, accessories — e.g. you may order MDF fronts somewhere other than the cabinets.
