Recommended materials and tools

Hardware, accessories and tools that come in handy when making your own furniture. The list will keep growing.

We don't link to specific shops or listings, because offers change quickly. The „Search on Google” button finds current offers. Before buying, check the dimensions, adjustment range and load capacity in the product description.

Legs and plinths

Häfele AXILO — adjustable furniture leg, H 80

Hardware · legs

An adjustable leg (foot) for floor-standing cabinets, e.g. kitchen units or an alcove unit. You level the cabinets on an uneven floor with the leg height, and at the front the legs are hidden by the plinth (bottom filler). The H 80 version is about 80 mm high.

In the designer: in the cabinet card, in the Legs section, set the height to 80 mm. The plinth adjusts its height itself (legs minus the gap). You'll find the number of legs to buy under 💾 Cut list… → Accessories.

Connectors and screws

Confirmat screws 6.4 × 50 mm

Hardware · connectors

Furniture screws for joining chipboard cabinets: sides to top/bottom panels, rails and fixed shelves. They are driven through the face of one board into the edge of the other. They hold firmly and can be undone and screwed in again, e.g. when moving house. The 50 mm length suits 18 mm board: 18 mm passes through the board and about 32 mm goes into the edge of the other one.

Holes for confirmat screws are drilled with a special stepped bit (a confirmat drill bit), which in one go drills the hole in both boards and the recess for the head. Choose the bit to match the screw diameter (6.4 mm). Confirmat screws are driven with an Allen key or a bit, depending on the head.

In the designer: in the cabinet card, in the Cabinet section, you set the Top panel / top rails and Bottom panel: between the sides or on top of / under the sides. That decides which board you screw the confirmats through. With top/bottom panels between the sides, the heads are visible on the outside of the sides, so on a side that's on show, cover caps in the board colour come in handy.

Tools

Häfele AXILO leg adjustment key

Tools · fitting

A key for adjusting the height of AXILO legs. It makes levelling cabinets that are already in place easier. One key is enough for the whole unit.
