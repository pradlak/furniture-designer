Kitchen design software — kitchen cabinets in 3D for free

Updated: 2026-11-27

Design made-to-measure kitchen cabinets and see them in 3D straight away: base units on legs, wall units, drawers, a worktop with a cut-out for the sink and the hob, a plinth. The program calculates the cut list, so you go straight from the design to ordering the boards.

What you can design

  • Base and wall units — any dimensions; wall units are placed at the height you choose.
  • Drawers and doors — drawers with a front or internal ones behind the doors, different heights, one above the other or with free space; the program fits their height to the space in the cabinet.
  • Worktop across the whole run, with a cut-out for the sink, a hole for the tap and a cut-out for an induction or gas hob (visible in 3D) and edge routing.
  • Plinth and fillers — the bottom filler hides the legs, side fillers even out the gaps at the wall.
  • Cooker hood and obstacles — mark a cooker hood, pipes or a chimney breast and the cabinets fit the free space.
  • Wall tiles — a strip of tiles above the worktop with an approximate tile count.

Standard kitchen cabinet dimensions

  • Worktop height: usually 85–92 cm (cabinet about 72–82 cm + legs/plinth + 28–38 mm worktop).
  • Base unit depth: about 56–58 cm for a 60 cm worktop.
  • Wall units: 30–35 cm deep, bottom edge about 50–60 cm above the worktop.
  • Widths: typically 30, 40, 45, 50, 60 and 80 cm; a sink unit is usually 60–80 cm.

The program is meant for designing cabinets and fronts made of boards for panel cutting — it does not replace planning the installations (electricity, water, gas) or choosing the appliances.

Frequently asked questions

Is the kitchen design software free?

Yes — it runs in your browser, with no installation, no sign-up and no fees.

Can I design a worktop with a sink and hob cut-out?

Yes. You can add a sink cut-out, a tap hole, cable grommets and a cut-out for an induction or gas hob to the worktop — the hob is visible in 3D and the cut-out dimensions go into the cut list.

How do I order the boards for kitchen cabinets?

Download the cut list in Excel or CSV (with edge banding and accessories) and order the cutting from a board supplier, or load the ready file on meble.pl (a Polish panel-cutting service).

Does the program design corner kitchens?

You place cabinets along the walls of the room, which you can shape freely (sloped walls too). There are no special corner cabinets with a carousel yet.
