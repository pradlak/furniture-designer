Privacy policy

Effective from 15 November 2026.

This is an English translation of the Polish privacy policy. In case of any discrepancy, the Polish version prevails.

Who processes the data

The data controller is Paweł Radlak Consulting (Polish tax ID / NIP 6612267387), operator of the meblesamemu.pl service. Contact regarding data: kontakt@meblesamemu.pl or the “Send feedback” form in the designer.

Using the service without an account

The designer works without signing in. The project is then saved only in your browser (browser storage, so-called localStorage) — it is not sent to our server. In the same way the browser remembers the program settings (e.g. the selection colour) and that the welcome window has already been closed.

Account (sign-in with Google)

If you sign in with Google to save projects in your account, we process:

  • your Google account ID, e-mail address, first name and profile picture address (from Google, with your consent given on the sign-in screen — the picture is used only to show the avatar in the corner of the page),
  • program settings (selection colour, units of measurement) — so that they work the same on every device,
  • the projects you save in your account, with their creation and modification dates,
  • sign-in session data (a random identifier in a cookie, the expiry date).

We do not store passwords — sign-in takes place at Google. We do not sell the data or share it for advertising purposes. Purpose: providing the project saving service (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Feedback form

A message from the “Send feedback” form is sent by e-mail to the controller together with the (optional) e-mail address you provide, information about your browser and country (to understand the report). Purpose: replying to the report and developing the program (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Visit statistics

We use the Umami tool for anonymous statistics (e.g. number of visits, cut list downloads). Umami does not use cookies and does not collect data that could identify you.

Cookies

We use only the strictly necessary sign-in session cookie (ms_session, valid for 30 days) and a short-lived cookie securing the sign-in process (ms_oauth_state, 10 minutes). Signing in does not work without them, so they do not require consent. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Where the data is kept and for how long

  • The service and the database run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), and sign-in — on Google. Data may also be processed outside the European Economic Area, on the basis of the mechanisms provided for in the GDPR (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses).
  • Account and projects — until the account is deleted. Sign-in sessions — up to 30 days.
  • Messages from the feedback form — for as long as necessary to handle the report.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict its processing, the right to data portability and the right to object. You can delete your account together with all projects yourself: in the designer, “☁ My projects” → “Delete account”. You can also save each project to a file with the “Export” button. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office in Poland (UODO, uodo.gov.pl).
