Sloped ceiling wardrobe — design made-to-measure attic furniture

Updated: 2026-11-27

Furniture under a sloped ceiling is something you cannot buy ready-made — every attic has a different knee wall and a different roof angle. In Meble Samemu you enter the slope dimensions and the program cuts the cabinets to the roof and calculates the angled pieces for the cut list.

How to design a wardrobe under a slope

  1. Choose an attic with a slope. When creating a new project choose “Attic” and enter the width, height and depth.
  2. Describe the slope. Enter the height of the knee wall (where the slope starts at the wall) and the length of the slope or its angle. The slope can be on the left, right, back or front.
  3. Add cabinets. A cabinet reaching under the slope gets a sloped top and sloped sides — the program makes sure nothing sticks out beyond the roof and leaves the clearance you set.
  4. Fronts and shelves. Doors under the slope are cut to the angle too; shelves fit the inside of the cabinet.
  5. Cut list. The list of boards includes angled cuts with dimensions (e.g. “cut corner 470 × 560 mm”), so the workshop knows exactly how to cut.

What to watch out for with attic furniture

  • Measure the slope in several places — rafters and plasterboard are rarely perfectly straight. Use the smallest dimensions in the project.
  • Leave clearance under the slope (about 10–20 mm) — a sloped cabinet has to be slid into place, and a filler covers the gap.
  • The low part at the knee wall is great for drawers or shoe shelves, the higher part — for a hanging rail and doors.
  • Depth: under a slope a shallower wardrobe (40–50 cm) often pays off, so the fronts are not too low.

Frequently asked questions

Will the program cut the cabinet to the slope automatically?

Yes. After you enter the knee wall and the slope, cabinets pushed under the roof get a sloped top and sides, and the cut list includes the angled cuts with dimensions.

What slope can I design?

Any angle — you enter the slope as the knee wall height and the slope length (or the angle). It can be on any side of the room, and several at once.

How much clearance should I leave under the slope?

Usually 10–20 mm. The program has a separate clearance setting for the slope and checks that the cabinet fits.

Is the design free?

Yes, the designer runs in your browser for free and without signing up.
