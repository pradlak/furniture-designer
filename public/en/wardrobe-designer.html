Online wardrobe designer — design a made-to-measure wardrobe for free

Updated: 2026-11-27

Meble Samemu is a free wardrobe design program that runs in your browser. Enter the size of the space, add cabinets, shelves, doors and drawers, view the wardrobe in 3D and finally download a ready cut list for panel cutting — the list you use to order cut boards.

How to design a wardrobe in the program

  1. Size of the space. Choose a rectangular alcove or an attic with a sloped ceiling and enter the width, height and depth. Add obstacles: a skirting board, a pipe, a chimney breast.
  2. Cabinets. Click “Add → Cabinet” — each new cabinet splits the alcove into equal parts. Change the width, height and depth with the mouse in 3D or by typing numbers.
  3. Interior and fronts. Add shelves, doors (single or double) and drawers — with a front or internal ones, hidden behind the doors. The program places the hinges and warns you when a door is too wide.
  4. Fillers and plinth. Side fillers cover the gaps at uneven walls, the top filler fills the space below the ceiling, and the bottom filler (plinth) hides the legs.
  5. Cut list. Download the list of boards to cut (Excel, CSV or a ready file for meble.pl, a Polish panel-cutting service) with edge banding and accessories: hinges, drawer runners, legs, handles.

Dimensions worth remembering

  • Depth of a clothes wardrobe: about 60 cm (a hanger is about 45–50 cm wide); a wardrobe for books or shoes — 30–40 cm.
  • Cabinet width: up to about 100 cm — wider shelves sag under load.
  • Door width: up to about 60 cm per leaf; wider openings are better as double doors.
  • Clearance below the ceiling: a few to a dozen or so millimetres, so the cabinet can be stood up; the top filler covers the gap.

Frequently asked questions

Is the wardrobe designer free?

Yes. The program runs in your browser, is completely free and needs no installation.

Do I need to sign up?

No. The project is saved in your browser and you can export it to a file. Signing in with Google is optional — it is useful when you want your projects on several computers.

How do I order the boards for a wardrobe?

Download the cut list (Excel or CSV) and take it to a panel-cutting service, or load the ready file on meble.pl (a Polish panel-cutting service). The list contains cutting dimensions, quantities and edge banding.

What board thickness should I use for a wardrobe?

Cabinets and shelves are usually made of 18 mm melamine-faced chipboard, the back of 3 mm HDF. In the program you choose the material and its thickness — the cut list is recalculated automatically.

Can the program design a wardrobe with sliding doors?

For now the program designs cabinets with hinged doors and drawers. For a sliding wardrobe you can design the cabinets and interior in it and order the sliding doors separately from a sliding door system.
